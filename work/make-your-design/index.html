Fashion moves fast.

Every customer has a brief, and the clock is ticking. Explore a colourful 3D town, collect the materials you need and head to the Design Studio to bring each outfit to life.

Choose your garment, experiment with colours, patterns, trims and cuts, then put your crafting skills to the test. Every choice shapes the finished design.

Deliver your creations to earn coins, build combos and keep the clock running. Upgrade your studio, discover rare designs and chase a new high score with every run.
