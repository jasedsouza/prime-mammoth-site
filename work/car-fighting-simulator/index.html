Car Fighting Simulator

Line it up. Hit the boost. Own the arena.

Smash through a five-round championship. Chase down rivals, land huge collisions and choose upgrades between victories. Earn REP to unlock more cars and take another shot at the title.

Play the game ↗How it works ↓

Desktop: WASD or arrows · Space to boost · R to recover · P to pauseMobile: Touch controls · Landscape

Every hit has a purpose.

Find your opening, line up your rival and hit the boost. Land heavy crashes, chain hits into combos and wreck the opposition before they wreck you.

Five rounds stand between you and the championship. Face a lone rival, survive outnumbered fights and take on heavier opposition before the final showdown.

Choose a temporary upgrade after each of the first four victories. Earn REP from round wins to unlock more cars in the garage, ready for your next run.
