Sandwich Panic

Making lunch the hard way.

Build a machine. Launch the ingredients. Hope they land in the right order.

Play the free demoHow it works

Sandwich Panic is a 3D physics puzzle game about turning a simple sandwich into a ridiculous engineering problem.

BrowserDesktop3 levelsFree demo

An unnecessarily complicated kitchen.

You're given a recipe, a kitchen full of machinery and a limited set of tools. Place springs, fans, ramps and flippers to guide ingredients onto your sandwich.

Get the angles right, work out the timing and see what happens. If your lunch flies across the kitchen instead, adjust your machine and try again.

Build. Test. Refine.

  1. Build

    Arrange your tools to create a working contraption.

  2. Test

    Fire up the machine and watch the physics unfold.

  3. Refine

    Adjust your setup until lunch comes together.

There's more than one way to make lunch.

Real physics. Ridiculous results.

Ingredients bounce, slide, tumble and collide. Small changes can make a big difference.

Your machine, your solution.

Experiment with different layouts, angles and timings. There's no single prescribed solution.

Three puzzles to get you started.

Take on Cheese Toastie, Tomato Trouble and The BLT Challenge in the free demo.
